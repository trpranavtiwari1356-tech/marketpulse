How this allocation is calculated

Risk parity, not equal money. Each stock's weight starts at 1 ÷ its volatility, so calmer stocks get more and jumpy ones get less — every holding contributes a similar share of portfolio risk.

Correlation penalty. Stocks that move too closely with the rest of the basket (average correlation > 0.7) are trimmed, since they add little diversification; that weight shifts to the more independent names.

Conviction tilt (optional). If you turn it on, your 1–10 score nudges weights up or down by a gentle 0.25× Kelly fraction — a tilt on top of the risk-parity base, never a replacement for it.

Position cap. No single stock exceeds your cap; any excess is spread across the rest. Share quantity = allocation ÷ live price. This is a starting point, not advice.
