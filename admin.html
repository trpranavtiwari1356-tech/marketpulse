How the numbers are counted

Visitor
One browser on one device. Someone on a phone and a laptop counts as two; clearing browser data or a private window also starts a new visitor.
New visitor
A visitor whose first-ever visit falls inside the selected period. Everyone else in the period is returning.
Visit
One browser tab’s session. It ends after 30 minutes without activity; a tab open under 2 seconds isn’t recorded.
Active time
Seconds the tab was on screen and used (a click, scroll, touch or key in the last 5 minutes). Time in a background tab or while idle is not counted.
Engaged visit
A visit with at least 10 seconds of active time, or one that opened 2 or more sections.
Compared with
The same window one period earlier: today so far vs yesterday up to the same time, last 7 days vs the 7 before, and so on.
Online now
Browsers that reported in during the last 2 minutes.
Location
Estimated from the internet provider’s IP address. Usually the right city or a nearby one; mobile networks can place people in a hub city.
Came from
Your link tag, or the referring website the browser reported. “Direct / app” means none was sent: a typed address, a bookmark, or a link opened from an app such as WhatsApp.
Your devices
Browsers marked “This is me” (any browser signed in here is marked automatically). They are left out everywhere unless “Include my devices” is ticked.
Time zone
All days, hours and times are Indian Standard Time.
Not counted
Search-engine crawlers, link-preview bots and automated browsers.
